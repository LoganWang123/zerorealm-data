# ZeroRealm Editorial Growth V11 Design

## Problem

The current content pipeline can collect enough material, but the public output still behaves like a broad news roundup. It over-connects generic AI news to smart cabinets, repeats topics across days, and asks operators to make speculative investments. The resulting WeChat drafts are not specific enough to earn opens, saves, or follows.

## Editorial Position

ZeroRealm serves one reader: the owner or operating lead of a smart-cabinet fleet. Each issue makes one promise:

> Understand one verified retail change and make one low-risk operating move.

The nightly report must select exactly one core event with a direct source URL and an observable link to assortment, sell-through, gross margin, inventory, replenishment, loss, site efficiency, or conversion. It may include at most two supporting signals. Generic AI, financing, personnel, or strategy stories are excluded unless the source states a direct frontline retail impact.

## Article Contract

- Title: concrete subject + verified change + operator implication; no `日报`, issue number, or clickbait prefix.
- Opening: within 120 Chinese characters, state the fact, affected metric, and first check.
- Body: fact, one-layer interpretation, one reversible action, one KPI, one stop condition.
- Length: 900–1400 Chinese characters.
- Sources: one direct source link per included event; no homepage or search links.
- Images: bright documentary retail operations; no fake screens, pseudo text, dark AI glow, or posed executive portraits.
- Publishing: create or update a WeChat draft only. Never send or publish automatically.

## Tonight's Issue

The core event is Eastroc Beverage's 2026 first-half result reported on 2026-07-30: revenue RMB 12.443 billion, up 15.89%, and net profit attributable to shareholders RMB 2.867 billion, up 20.72%. The operator takeaway is not to copy a national brand's portfolio, but to inspect beverage sell-through, stockouts, and slow movers during peak season before changing the assortment.

The action is a 10-cabinet, 7-day beverage shelf audit:

1. Rank beverage SKUs by seven-day units and gross-profit contribution.
2. Replenish the top quartile before adding new SKUs.
3. Remove one slow mover only when its 14-day units and gross-profit contribution are both below the fleet median.

## Growth Loop

The article ends with one reader prompt tied to their own operation, inviting a compact reply such as cabinet count and beverage stockout rate. Tomorrow's follow-up can use aggregated replies only after manual review. No fabricated readership, monitoring, or performance claims are allowed.
